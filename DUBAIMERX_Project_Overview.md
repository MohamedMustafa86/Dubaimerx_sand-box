# DUBAIMERX Platform - Project Overview

## 🌟 About the Project

**DUBAIMERX** is a comprehensive B2B e-commerce platform designed to connect Gulf excellence with global markets. The platform serves as a digital marketplace specifically focused on promoting premium non-oil products from the UAE and GCC region to international buyers.

## 🎯 Platform Mission & Vision

### Mission
To create a comprehensive digital marketplace that showcases the quality and diversity of Gulf products while facilitating seamless international trade relationships.

### Vision
To become the premier global platform for Gulf trade, fostering economic growth and international partnerships.

## 🏗️ Technical Architecture

### Frontend Framework
- **React 18.2.0** with modern hooks and functional components
- **Vite** as the build tool for fast development and optimized production builds
- **React Router DOM** for client-side routing

### Styling & UI
- **Tailwind CSS** for utility-first styling
- **Radix UI** components for accessible, customizable UI primitives
- **Framer Motion** for smooth animations and transitions
- **Lucide React** for modern iconography

### Backend Integration
- **Supabase** for authentication and backend services
- Custom Supabase client configuration for enhanced functionality

### Internationalization
- Multi-language support: **English**, **Arabic**, and **French**
- RTL (Right-to-Left) support for Arabic
- Comprehensive translation system with context providers

## 🏪 Product Categories

The platform supports 9 major product categories:

1. **Consumer Products** - Perfumes, cosmetics, cleaning supplies, personal care
2. **Food Products** - Dates, dairy, juices, canned/frozen foods, spices, oils
3. **Building Materials** - Ceramics, porcelain, industrial glass, sanitary ware, aluminum, iron
4. **Industrial Products** - Pipes, cables, electrical equipment, plastic products, filters
5. **Tech & Smart Products** - Surveillance devices, smart home solutions, SaaS applications
6. **Medical & Health** - Medicines, supplements, protective equipment, medical packaging
7. **Packaging Products** - Containers, wrapping materials, labels, eco-friendly packaging
8. **Furniture & Furnishings** - Wood/metal furniture, hotel furnishings, mattresses, carpets
9. **Sustainable Products** - Biodegradable products, solar systems, recycling solutions
